THE AUTHOR & THE OPERATING ARCHITECTURE SERIES

Designed to be used.

Solarian is by JB Bullock, an independent researcher and operating executive. The project examines the unglamorous but necessary infrastructure of a civilisation that no longer lives on one planet.

Why an operator would design a calendar.

Operating architectures organise complex systems into intelligible, repeatable units. Solarian applies that approach to civil time across the Solar System—connecting everyday coordination to the realities of planetary motion.

The emphasis is deliberately practical: explain the architecture openly, make it testable and allow independent builders to assess or implement it.
