THE CENTRAL IDEA

The year belongs to the orbit.
The mear belongs to people.

Our current calendar grew up on one planet. Solarian asks what it would look like if humanity designed an operating calendar with the entire Solar System in mind.

Its answer is not a single planetary year imposed everywhere. It is a shared language of weeks, minths, mears and locally meaningful orbital calendars.
