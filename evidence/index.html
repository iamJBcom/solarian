PROVENANCE & CONFORMANCE

Evidence, not assertion.

Solarian's supporting calculations distinguish verified boundaries from provisional profiles, model assumptions and outstanding source-data checks. Those distinctions are retained here rather than concealed behind a single “complete” label.

DE440 synchronized planetary profile checksTESTED

Year-boundary and registered-form evidence for Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, with documented model and time-window limits.

Venus and Mercury independent-regime chronologyTESTED

Real astronomical crossing evaluations and CSPICE parity; Mercury's angular month-phase representation is specialised.

Phase-fidelity applicability thresholdsAUTHOR RATIFIED

At most 30° standard; above 30° through 45° marginal review; above 45° requires specialised astronomical treatment.
